This is a Friday Night Funkin' (FNF) mod based on the Neon Genesis Evangelion universe. Development is almost complete, bringing players a brand-new synchronization and unexpected twists.

🌟 Key Features

• Character-Specific Weeks Included
Ray Week: Feel the hidden rhythm within a mysterious and calm atmosphere.
Asuka Week: Tsundere charm explosion! Intense and fast-paced tracks await you.
Kaoru Week: Experience sorrowful yet sophisticated charts that capture a deep bond with Shinji.
Mari Week: Cheerful personality on! Listen to the bright and lively charts.
• 🎂 Birthday Easter Egg
	• A special celebration event is hidden and will trigger when specific conditions (or specific dates) are met. Don't miss out and try to find it!
• 🚫 Powerful Anti-Cheat System
	• "Anta baka?!" If you dare manipulate the game files or use cheats, Asuka herself will step in to give you a spicy warning and teach you a hard lesson. (You'd better just join the Cafe community quietly.)

🕹️ How to Play

1. Download the game and extract the archive.
2. Run PsychEngine.exe.
3. Choose your desired week in Story Mode or Free Play and enjoy!
